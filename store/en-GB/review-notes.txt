Purpose and audience: Puzzles by Jo Clegg is a free collection of eight logic and word puzzles: Sudoku (classic and killer), Klondike Solitaire, Two Tone, Sightlines, Five Letters, Kakuro, Slide Out and Word Wheel. It is for adults and older children who enjoy short puzzles. Once its puzzle banks have downloaded, it plays fully offline. It has no ads, tracking or in-app purchases. The same games are also on Android and the web (https://puzzles.joclegg.dev).

Setup and access: No account is needed. Every game works straight after launch. Signing in is optional and only syncs saved games, scores and settings across devices. Sign-in is through Sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft, so no demo credentials are needed: the reviewer can use their own Apple ID. Signed-in users can delete their account in the app from the account screen (Delete account), which deletes their account and all their synced data.

External services: puzzle banks and word lists are downloaded from GitHub Pages (joclegg.github.io/puzzles-data). Supabase handles authentication and storage of synced games. Apple, Google and Microsoft provide sign-in (OAuth). No AI, payment or analytics services are used.

Regional differences: none. The app works the same in all regions. Word games use an English word list.

Regulated or third-party material: not applicable. All puzzles are generated by the developer. The app uses no third-party IP and is not in a regulated industry.
